CAGR calculator

Enter a starting value, an ending value and either the number of years or two dates. The calculator returns the compound annual growth rate (CAGR) and the total return.

How it works

CAGR = (ending value / starting value) ^ (1 / years) - 1. It is the single steady yearly rate that would turn the starting value into the ending value.

Example

Growing $10,000 to $15,000 over 4 years is (1.5) ^ (1/4) - 1, about 10.67% a year.

Questions

Is CAGR the same as average return?

No. An average of yearly returns ignores compounding and usually overstates growth when returns are volatile.

Does CAGR show risk?

No. Two investments can share a CAGR and have very different swings along the way.
