Stock profit and loss calculator

Enter the number of shares, the buy price, the sell price and any total fees. The calculator returns your cost, proceeds, profit or loss and percentage return.

How it works

Profit = (shares x sell price - fees) - (shares x buy price). The percentage return is profit divided by cost.

Example

Buying 100 shares at $50 and selling at $58.50 with no fees gives $850 profit on a $5,000 cost, a 17.0% return.

Questions

Does this include taxes?

No. Taxes depend on your situation and are not included.

Can I use it for a loss?

Yes. Enter a sell price below your buy price and the result is negative.

For illustration only. This is not financial advice, and real returns vary and can be negative.
