Savings goal calculator

Enter a target, what you have now, an assumed annual return and the number of years. The calculator returns the monthly amount to save and how long your current savings rate would take.

How it works

It converts the annual return to a monthly rate, grows your current balance forward, and solves for the monthly payment that covers the remaining gap.

Example

To reach $100,000 in 10 years starting with $10,000 at 6% a year, you would save roughly $505 a month.

Questions

What if returns are lower than assumed?

You would need to save more or wait longer. Try a lower rate to see the effect.

Are contributions at the start or end of the month?

The calculator assumes the end of each month.

For illustration only. This is not financial advice, and real returns vary and can be negative.
