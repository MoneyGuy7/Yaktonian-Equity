Position size calculator

Enter your account size, the percentage you are willing to risk on one trade, your entry price and your stop price. The calculator returns the number of shares to buy.

How it works

Risk per trade = account size x risk percentage. Shares = risk per trade / (entry price - stop price), rounded down.

Example

With a $25,000 account risking 1%% ($250), an entry of $50 and a stop of $47, you risk $3 a share, so you buy 83 shares.

Questions

Does a stop-loss guarantee my loss is capped?

No. Prices can gap past a stop, so a loss can be larger than planned.

What risk percentage is typical?

That is a personal choice. Many traders keep it to a small fraction of the account per trade.

For illustration only. This is not financial advice, and real returns vary and can be negative.
