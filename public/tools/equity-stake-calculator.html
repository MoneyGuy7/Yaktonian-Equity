Equity stake calculator

Enter how much you plan to invest and the current portfolio value. The calculator shows the share of the portfolio you would own and what that share could be worth at different returns.

How it works

Your stake is your investment divided by the portfolio value after you invest: stake = amount / (portfolio value + amount). Because your money is added to the pool, the denominator includes it.

Example

Investing $10,000 into a $190,000 portfolio gives you $10,000 / $200,000 = 5.0% of the portfolio.

Questions

Does my percentage stay the same over time?

Only if nobody else adds or withdraws money. New deposits dilute your share even though your dollar value is unchanged.

Is the return figure a forecast?

No. It is an assumption you choose to illustrate a scenario.

For illustration only. This is not financial advice, and real returns vary and can be negative.
